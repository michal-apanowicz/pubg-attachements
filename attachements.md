# Attachements

## Cechy

Praktyczne wpływy poszczególnych cech na zachowanie broni w grze i rónice:

- Spread: Określa, jak bardzo pociski rozpraszają się podczas strzału.
- Deviation: Określa odchylenie pocisku od zamierzonego celu.
- Recoil: Określa siłę odrzutu broni po wystrzale.
- Sway: Określa, jak bardzo broń porusza się podczas celowania.
- Camera DOF: Określa głębię ostrości kamery podczas celowania.

## Dolna szyna montaowa (uchwyty + laser)

- Angled Foregrip (AR, SMG, DMR)
  The angled foregrip reduces horizontal recoil at the cost of increased sway as well as slightly increasing the recoil animation of the weapon itself. It is therefore best suited for weapons with a high rate of fire or heavy horizontal recoil, and in close to mid-ranged combat.

* 20.00% Recoil Pattern Scale
* 15.00% Horizontal Recoil

- 10% ADS Speed
- 20% Weapon Sway
- 5% Animation Kick

* Half Grip
  The Half Grip reduces vertical and horizontal recoil by a small amount and improves recoil recovery time, with a penalty to weapon stability and sway. It is therefore ideal for weapons with a high rate of fire in close-range combat.

- 20% Recoil Pattern Scale
- 8% Vertical Recoil
- 8% Horizontal Recoil

* 10% Recoil Recovery
* 20% Weapon Sway
* 15% Animation Kick

- Light Grip
  The lightweight grip is the only grip to not affect the vertical or horizontal recoil multipliers directly, instead decreasing the recoil animation and sway of a weapon. It is thus suited to weapons that have a strong recoil animation but low camera recoil, such as the G36C, or single shot weapons like the SKS.

* 20% Recoil Pattern Scale
* 20% Animation Kick
* 20% Weapon Sway

- Thumb Grip
  The Thumb Grip decreases the recoil pattern and weapon sway by 20%, vertical recoil by 5% as well as decreasing the time it takes to aim down the sights by 30%. This grip is therefore ideal in conjunction with the more powerful scopes, as the reduced sway allows for easier shot placement and the increased ADS speed negates the reduction that comes with the stronger optics. The thumb grip's significant ADS speed improvement also stacks with the increased ADS speed of all optics weaker than the 4x ACOG.

* 5% Vertical Recoil
* 20% Recoil Pattern Scale
* 20% weapon sway

- 30% ADS speed

* Vertical Foregrip
  The Vertical Foregrip reduces vertical recoil by 15% and recoil pattern by 20%. It is therefore best suited for weapons with high vertical recoil or for long-distance shooting where precision is more of a concern over volume of fire.
  -20.00% Recoil Pattern Scale

- 15.00% Vertical Recoil

* Laser Sight
  reduces the bullet deviation that occurs during hip-fire and target-aiming, thus making it a go-to attachment for run and gun moments
  -30% Deviation
  -70% Deviation for Handguns

## Przyrządy celownicze

- Red Dot Sight
  Compared to the holographic sight, the red dot sight has a less bulkier frame, although the reticle is somewhat blurrier.
  +20.00% ADS Speed

- Holographic Sight
  At 100 meters, a standing person will fit within the outer circle of the reticle, allowing for quick rangefinding.
  +20.00% ADS Speed

- 2x Aimpoint Scope
  a standing person 100 meters away will fit perfectly between the top and bottom of the outer circle, and a standing person 200 meters away will fit between the center dot and the outer circle. At 300 meters, the width of a standing person will be obscured by the center dot.
  2x Magnification (40° FOV)
  +10.00% ADS speed

- 3x Backlit Scope
  The reticle can be used as a rangefinder for targets between 100 meters out to 400 meters by using the shoulder width of a target and matching it up with the horizontal lines
  3x Magnification (26.66° FOV)
  +10.00% ADS speed

- 4x ACOG Scope
  As of the Miramar update, the 4x, along with Red Dot Sight and Holographic Sight, no longer have adjustable zeroing distance, setting the gun to a fixed 100 meter zero.

- 6x Scope
  The reticle also features a stadiametric rangefinder suitable for standing targets between 300 to 800 meters. This scope is very versatile due to its simple rangefinder and ability to zoom out to 3x magnification, as well as its compatibility with assault rifles and submachine guns, greatly extending the range at which opponents can be attacked at.
  -20.00% ADS speed

- 8x CQBSS Scope
  Due to its rarity and usefulness, this attachment should be taken whenever possible, although when not fixed to a weapon it does take up a fair amount of inventory space - as much as a gas can
  8x Magnification (10° FOV)
  -20% ADS speed[1]
